## Review before posting

- Captions are based on automatic speech recognition, compared with a second recognizer, **not verified by a person listening**. Please listen and correct them. Editing an SRT does not change an already-burned caption; rerender from the word data in the rebuild kit to change it.
- Third-party footage and any embedded music remain subject to the owners' rights. **Reuse permission has not been verified**; obtain the necessary clearance before publishing.
- All 15 MP4s passed a full video/audio decode and SRT time-bound checks. Final loudness measured from **−16.90 to −15.27 LUFS**, with highest measured true peak **−1.89 dBTP**. Visual review used representative frames, not a full human watch. See [QC details](https://github.com/dreamer2664/competitionvideo28/blob/main/synchromora/edits/QC.md).
