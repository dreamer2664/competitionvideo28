## 1. Primary & Peer-Reviewed Bibliography

1. **[S1] Freeth, T., Bitsakis, Y., Moussas, X., Seiradakis, J. H., Tselikas, A., Mangou, H., Zafeiropoulou, M., Hadland, R., Bate, D., Ramsey, A., Allen, M., Crawley, A., Hockley, P., Malzbender, T., Gelb, D., Ambrisco, W., & Edmunds, M. G. (2006).**
   *"Decoding the ancient Greek astronomical calculator known as the Antikythera Mechanism."*
   **Nature**, 444(7119), 587–591. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature05357
   - **Role:** Primary authoritative source for the 2005 X-Tek 450 kV micro-focus X-ray CT scan ("BladeRunner"), Hewlett-Packard Polynomial Texture Mapping (PTM), the 30 surviving bronze gears across 82 fragments, the corrected Metonic/sidereal gear train (`b2=64, c1=38, c2=48, d1=24, d2=127, e2=32`), the 223-tooth Saros gear (`e3`), and the Hipparchus pin-and-slot lunar anomaly mechanism (`k1, k2`).

2. **[S2] Freeth, T., Jones, A., Steele, J. M., & Bitsakis, Y. (2008).**
   *"Calendars with Olympiad display and eclipse prediction on the Antikythera Mechanism."*
   **Nature**, 454(7204), 614–617. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature07130
   - **Role:** Primary source for the Corinthian family of month names on the 235-month Metonic spiral (*Phoinikaios, Kraneios, Lanotropios, Machaneus...*), the four-year Panhellenic Games (Olympiad) dial, and the 223-month Saros eclipse prediction glyphs (`Σ` = *Selene* / lunar, `Η` = *Helios* / solar) plus the 54-year Exeligmos 8-hour shift dial.

3. **[S3] Price, D. J. de Solla (1974).**
   *"Gears from the Greeks: The Antikythera Mechanism — A Calendar Computer."*
   **Transactions of the American Philosophical Society**, New Series, Vol. 64, Part 7, 1–70. https://doi.org/10.2307/1006146
   - **Role:** Seminal historical and radiographic monograph detailing the 1900 sponge-diver discovery under Captain Dimitrios Kontos and diver Elias Stadiatis at ~42 m depth off Antikythera, Valerios Stais's May 17, 1902 identification of gear wheels in Athens, and the first 2D gamma/X-ray radiography with Charalambos Karakalos.

4. **[S4] Freeth, T., Higgon, D., Dacanalis, A., MacDonald, L., Georgakopoulou, M., & Wojcik, A. (2021).**
   *"A Model of the Cosmos in the Antikythera Mechanism."*
   **Scientific Reports** (Nature Portfolio), 11, 5821. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-84310-w
   - **Role:** Primary source for the 2021 UCL reconstruction of the front planetary display, specifically the newly decoded front-plate synodic period inscriptions `(289, 462)` for Venus (289 synodic cycles in 462 tropical years) and `(427, 442)` for Saturn (427 synodic cycles in 442 tropical years), using the 63-tooth gear in Fragment D.

5. **[S5] Jones, A. (2017).**
   *A Portable Cosmos: Revealing the Antikythera Mechanism, Scientific Wonder of the Ancient World.*
   Oxford University Press. ISBN 978-0199739349.
   - **Role:** Secondary scholarly cross-reference for Hellenistic astronomical background (Meton of Athens, Hipparchus of Nicaea's lunar theory, Parapegma star-calendar almanacs, and date range ~150–60 BCE).
